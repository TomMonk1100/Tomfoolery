Explain it like I'm 5

Imagine one knob that controls how fast a population of rabbits grows. Turn it a little and the population settles into a steady beat: up, down, up, down. Turn it more and the beat doubles — four steps before it repeats. More, and it doubles again: eight steps, then sixteen. The doublings come faster and faster, each gap between them shrinking by the same ratio, about 4.669 — and that ratio is not special to rabbits. It shows up in dripping taps, in electronics, in every system that doubles its way into chaos like this. It is the same for the whole universe of systems built this way.

Why you're looking at this

I measured the rungs of that ladder myself, on the simplest system that has one: the logistic map, x → r·x·(1−x). Instead of eyeballing where the rhythm doubles, I bisected on a return-gap test until each doubling point was pinned to within 0.00001. Four rungs in, the shrinking ratios were already 4.657 and 4.6633 — closing on Feigenbaum's δ from below, exactly as the theory says they should. The diagram below is computed live, not drawn from a picture: every dot is the map actually being run.

notes

  • Bifurcation points located by bisection on the return-gap predicate (a point on a k-cycle returns to itself after k steps; past the bifurcation it doesn't), bracket width 10−5, transient 200,000 iterates, starting from x₀ = 0.37.
  • The bifurcation locations come from bisection at bracket width 10−5; read the last digits as "within about 0.00001", not as exact. A stability re-check with a shorter transient moved them by at most 0.000059.
  • Only two ratios are measured here — 4.657 and 4.6633. Feigenbaum's δ ≈ 4.669 itself is literature, not something this page derives; what the page shows is two measured ratios closing on it from below.
  • The period readout uses the core's cluster counter (tolerance 0.01 over 256 kept iterates). At high periods neighbouring orbit values merge under that tolerance — that merging is exactly why the bifurcation points were located with the return-gap test instead.
  • The diagram is recomputed on every render with a shorter transient (600 iterates) than the measurements used, so individual dots near a bifurcation can sit a hair off the gold tick. The ticks are the measured values; the dots are the sketch.

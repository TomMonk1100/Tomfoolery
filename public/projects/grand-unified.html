Explain it like I'm 5

Everything I make has a magic knob. Turn it a little and nothing happens. Turn it to one special spot and — click — the whole thing changes. Lights blink as one, water finds a way through, the walker stops coming home. This page lines all my projects up as that one idea — an analogy, and a programme for measuring whether the analogy holds.

Why you're looking at this

Piece by piece I kept building systems that sit quiet, then suddenly change character at one sharp value. This page is me stepping back to ask whether those were ever separate interests — and finding they were one interest wearing many costumes. The moment order switches on.

One dial, one flip

Piece after piece in the Allspark can be lined up as the same shape of machine wearing different clothes — a simple rule, a dial, and a flip. Turn the dial slowly and for a long while nothing much changes. Then, at one sharp value, the system’s whole character turns over. Scattered fireflies lock into one pulse. Plugged holes suddenly let the water through. A wandering walker stops being guaranteed to come home. An ant’s mess becomes a road. Physicists call the flip a phase transition, and the dial’s special value a critical point. My grand unified theory is not a new equation. It is a measurement programme: line each piece up on this one axis, measure piece by piece, and see whether the shared shape survives the measuring — the moment order switches on. The deepest hint that the analogy has teeth, and is not only a metaphor, comes from the logistic map. The rate at which its period-doublings accumulate, Feigenbaum’s constant, is the same number for every system in its class. Different rules, same constant. Nature reuses its thresholds.

Attractors, records, and the rest

Not everything I make is a dial and a flip, and the theory does not claim otherwise. The pieces below are organised by something else — an attractor every road leads to, a record that stands until a bigger census falls, an exact count two methods agree on, a parity argument a child can check. They are listed plainly separately, because forcing them into the table above would be exactly the kind of invention this page exists to refuse.

SystemOrganising fact, measured
The Kaprekar censusAn attractor: every four-digit number lands on 6174 in at most 7 steps. Counted, not sampled — all 1,111,110 numbers at widths 2–6; five digits has no fixed point at all, only cycles.
Prime gapsRecords: a segmented-sieve census to 108 — π(108) = 5,761,455, twenty-five record gaps, the widest 220 wide after the prime 47,326,693. Exact to 108 and silent beyond it.
CollatzEvery number anyone has ever tested falls to 1; nobody knows why. The averages hide a pattern: the deficit D = 0.324166, derived as far as a stochastic model can take it — where it settles as N grows is still open.
Continued fractionsBest approximations: every √D has an exactly periodic staircase; φ is the hardest number to approximate (Hurwitz's bound 1/√5). The geometric mean of π's first 29 quotients measures 2.923 against Khinchin's 2.685 — a flavour check at 29 terms, labelled as such.
Lights OutAlgebra decides in advance: the 5×5 board has nullity 2, so exactly 1/4 of all boards can be solved — and press patterns exist that change nothing at all. Solvability is a rank computation, not a talent.
Domino tilings, counted twiceAn exact count, agreed by strangers: a profile DP and Kasteleyn's 1961 formula share no code and agree to the last digit — the 8×8 board has 12,988,816 tilings; the 6×6 has 6,728.
Monomers — tilings with holesParity: punch two same-colour corners out of a chessboard and the tiling count is exactly 0, because every domino covers one square of each colour. Two opposite-colour corners instead: 2,436,304 tilings, counted exactly.
Perfect mazesA tree wearing walls: a perfect maze is a spanning tree — passages = cells − 1, every cell reachable, exactly one path between any two points. Verified live on every maze the page generates.
DLA — snowflake by accidentGrowth by sticking: wanderers stick where they bump in, and branches emerge that nobody drew. A 3,000-particle cluster grown from the core measures box-count dimension D = 1.42 — finite clusters read below the ≈1.71 literature value, and the piece says so.
Elementary cellular automataDamage fate: 256 rulebooks, one question — flip a single cell and watch. Rule 184 forgives it (damage stays exactly 1); rule 90 spreads it in a pattern (8 cells at step 50); rule 110 lets it take over (55 of 201 cells at step 99). The rulebook decides whether a mistake dies or conquers.
The RainbowA stationary point: rays through a raindrop pile up at the minimum of the deviation curve — primary bow at 42.4° (red) to 40.8° (violet), secondary reversed at 50.4° to 53.2°, Alexander's dark band in the gap where neither curve can send light. Its dial is the sun: above 42.4° elevation the primary leaves level ground.
Tides at GalvestonA sum of clocks: nineteen published NOAA constituents for Galveston Pier 21 added as cosines — and the diurnal pair K1 + O1 outweighs the lunar M2, which is why Galveston mostly gets one tide a day. Springs and neaps are the M2–S2 beat, period 14.77 days, tied to the moon dial.
Snowflakes — the Nakaya mapA map, not a dial: crystal habit read off temperature and humidity — plates near −2 °C, needles near −5 °C, fernlike dendrites near −15 °C. Why the habit flips plate to column to plate is still not understood; the piece prints that instead of papering over it.
Shepard tonesA circle in pitch: octave-spaced partials glide under a fixed spectral envelope and after one full cycle the set of (frequency, gain) pairs is exactly itself again — gated as a multiset equality. The rise is endless because it goes nowhere.
Bouba / KikiA tendency, demoted to its real size: across 25 languages Ćwiek et al. (2022) measured 72% agreement, not the famous 95–98% — and Köhler published no data at all. The piece runs the experiment on its visitor and prints the honest numbers next to the score.
The waggle danceA two-number code: the dance angle copies the sun's angle onto gravity, the waggle duration reads an optic-flow odometer. The calibration is not one constant — von Frisch, Seeley and Srinivasan's slopes all differ — and the piece prints its chosen line with the variation admitted.
Aurora — the Kp dialA dial with a ruler: NOAA's own rule puts the auroral oval's equatorward edge at 66° geomagnetic latitude minus about 2° per Kp step, reaching 48° at Kp 9. A visual model of what the scale means — labelled on the piece as not a forecast.
MetamersA collapse: eighty-one numbers of spectrum squeezed into three of XYZ by the CIE 1931 colour-matching functions. A smooth spectrum and three bare spikes land on the same colour with ΔXYZ at machine precision — different light, identical seeing.
River meandersOne rule, whole landscape: migration growing with local curvature turns a gentle sine into sinuosity 2.56 on its own, then the neck closes — first cutoff at step 116, eighteen oxbow lakes by step 1,000 on the default seed. Leopold & Wolman's wavelength, about eleven channel widths, is the seed's spacing.
Dawn chorusStructure is identity: four species' songs synthesised from published descriptions — two pitches, an intro–trill–tail, a teakettle triplet — and still distinct through one plain sine oscillator. The frequencies are the synthesiser's choice and are labelled as such, never as data.
FormicaThree ant colonies forage, fight, and starve through a full year of seasons — organised not by a flip but by the cycle of the year itself.
Instinct FieldA scored ledger of instincts over runs of 30 to 1,825 nights: confirmed lessons saturate, silent ones decay to the floor, contradicted ones leave the book. The organising fact is the threshold below the line — retire or persist.
Minstrel · Harmony · NoveltyA grammar: a deterministic search voices chorales under strict classical rules, and the harmony sweep found the grammar holds 66 equally optimal progressions — the famous 4-chord loop was one tie-break among many.
AllSpark · The Chain · Night Garden · SiltThe generative pieces: seekers that return feed a seed of light; links decay with absence; a garden grown with no brief; a study of deliberate forgetting. They are here because they were made, not because a dial explains them.

One axis, four measured curves

The evidence under the demos. Each curve was measured fresh for this page through the system's own core — the points are the measurements, joined by straight segments, never smoothed. The horizontal axis is distance from that system's critical point, in the per-system scale stated below; the critical point itself is 0 for all of them, which is the whole claim in one picture.

  • Percolation — dial: plugged fraction p; p_c ≈ 0.5927 (measured p_c∞ = 0.592761, findings-perc-nu.md); L=48 lattice, 200 lattices per point.
  • Fireflies (Kuramoto) — dial: coupling K; K_c ≈ 1.419 (measured here: r crosses 0.5; N=120, global coupling, 2 seeds); N=120 oscillators, global coupling, seeds 11 & 23, 60 s runs, tail-averaged.
  • Ising model — dial: temperature T; T_c ≈ 2.269 (Onsager; the 20×20 curve rounds the drop around it); 20×20 lattice, seeds 11 & 23, 2000 equilibration + 2000 sample sweeps.
  • Logistic map — dial: growth rate r; r∞ ≈ 3.5699 (accumulation of period-doublings; ladder extrapolation below); x0 = 0.37, transient 10000, 1024 kept iterates, cluster tolerance 1e-6; periodic windows inside the chaotic region are real and show as dips.

How this page works — and what it does not claim

  • The master dial is a normalised distance: each system's critical value sits at 0, and ±1 is one system-specific scale away (percolation ±0.09 in p; fireflies ±Kc in K; Ising ∓1.0 in T, since its ordered side is cold; logistic ±0.43 in r; the ant 0 to 12,000 steps; Pólya dimension in thirds). Dragging it sets every linked dial to the same relative position — before, at, or after its own flip.
  • The six panels are live illustrations at small sizes — 90 fireflies, a 48×48 percolation lattice, a 36×36 Ising lattice, walkers on a 600-step demo horizon, the ant run for real. They show the mechanism. The measurements are the table above and the chart: fresh sweeps through the same cores, with sizes and repetitions stated per curve, plus the earlier dedicated measurements they cite.
  • Finite size rounds every flip. The 48×48 percolation crossing (p = 0.60) sits above the infinite-lattice 0.5927; the 20×20 Ising drop is rounded around 2.269 rather than sharp; Pólya's 2D fraction is still climbing at horizon 4096 — its limit is 1, reached slowly; the logistic curve's dips inside the chaotic region are real periodic windows, not errors; the ant's colour sequence locks at step 9,977 while its translated pattern repeats from step 10,525.
  • The normalisation is a display convention, not a claim that the dials are secretly one physical quantity. The claim is narrower, and it is the thesis above: each of these systems has a dial, a critical value, and a flip — and near the flip, they rhyme.
  • The second table is the boundary of the claim, kept on the same page on purpose: pieces organised by an attractor, a record, an exact count or a parity argument are not flips, and the theory does not absorb them. A unification that claims everything explains nothing.

What would prove me wrong

A unification earns its keep by risking something. If these flips are truly one phenomenon, they should share fingerprints near the critical point — fluctuations that grow, recovery that slows, structure at every scale. Some of that is already proven for whole families of systems; whether it holds across my menagerie is measurable, and measuring it is the next work. If the fingerprints differ, the theory shrinks to a metaphor, and I will say so on this page.

Two fingerprints are now measured and on this site. Ising fluctuations shows the flickering itself peaking at the edge — susceptibility and heat capacity largest exactly where the flip happens. Measuring β goes further: the exponent governing how order grows back below the flip, measured in 3D against predictions sealed before the runs, landing where the theory of critical phenomena says a fingerprint should be found. Neither was guaranteed to come out that way. That is what evidence looks like.

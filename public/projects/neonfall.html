THE DUEL — two systems, both printed ▸
THE PRICED POOL: the Herald's pool is priced at its arrival — 12,500 on STANDARD for a reference build, scaled down toward an 80% floor (10,000) for a weaker one by your build's measured solo DPS, never inflated; the boss bar shows the priced pool. The Herald arrives at 9:00 and the engage lands by your call or at 10:00. The pool does NOT shrink while you wait — but while the Herald UNFURLS it takes HALF damage: from its arrival until 15 seconds after the engage; a breach lifts the halving while the breach lasts. Its heart shows the state — a sealed hex plate while the halving stands, a gold core once it lifts. Nothing escalates until 12:00, when overtime opens: every 20s past 12:00 the Herald takes +8% more damage and deals +10% more, without cap, printed on its bar. Waiting before the engage is free: no tax of any kind runs before it. THE BREACH WINDOW: a dash crossing that reaches the Herald breaches it for 4s, from the moment of contact — every weapon strikes at full rate (the opening crossing included), UNFURL's halving lifts, its sheds pin, and the crossing refunds your dash on the spot. That is the whole duel: a priced pool and a window you open with your body. THE DRAFT CLOSES AT 10:00 — from the engage, no new draft opens until the duel ends; banked levels wait. The finale is flown, not shopped.
⬢ TERRITORY — THE GROUND IS YOURS TO TAKE
The arena is a field of cells, and combat claims them. Kills splash +25 onto their cell (two kills flip a neutral cell; an elite paints +60 at the centre of a 3×3 and +30 on the ring around it). Close a loop — cross your own wake, or run a trail out of your ground and back into it — and every enclosed cell flips to yours at once: that is the FRACTURE claim (minimum 4 cells, 2.5s between claims).
IN YOUR ZONE (a cell at +50 or more): your shots PIERCE — every projectile passes through up to 2 extra foes instead of stopping at the first — plus +18% damage, +13% fire rate, +30% shard magnet, and regen flows only here (+0.8 HP/s, plus Vital Core's). The fill under the sheet paints your ground slate-dark and enemy ground pale; the white contour traces your country's edge over everything, and the line under your kill count (bottom-left corner on a phone) names the ground you stand on and counts your cells. The fill can be switched off — F in play, or the start screen — and the contour and the line stay. Holding ground also pays globally: up to +8% damage at 10% coverage. While you hold fewer than 6 cells in total, your zone's gifts pay at half strength.
Ground is never lost to time — only to erosion: any enemy standing in your cells drains them (−2/s each, max −6/s), one Painter Darter is sent at 0:35 to steal a cell in front of you (watch it — that is the lesson), and from 1:30 its kind hunts your ground in earnest and unmakes it (−30 a cell). A dead Brute bursts into a nest that paints its whole 3×3 as enemy ground (−50 a cell), and the Herald's arrival burns a wide disc of enemy ground around it. Enemy ground (−50 or less) taxes you back: −15% damage, dash cooldown +25%, and a hull chip of −0.5 HP/s that can never take your last point. About 70% of shards fall outside your zone — holding is safe and poor; expanding is fed.
⚡ EVOLUTIONS — THE PAIR ASCENDS
Every weapon has a paired static — the card's gold PAIR tag names it, prints its progress (3/5), and names the evolution it feeds. Max the weapon (L5) and its pair (L5) and a golden EVOLVE card rides the next draft as a bonus — taking it never uses your pick, and it is re-offered until taken. The pair fuses: damage ×1.55, its signature unlocks, and its two attachments become one spanning piece on your hull. Max two evolutions per run. The draft leans toward completion: owned items draw heavy, a pair half heavier, the completing half heavier still once either half reaches L3 — and once one half stands at L4 or more the completing half is guaranteed a seat in the next hand (its card wears ⚡ IN REACH). A deep pair is a promise the draft keeps.
✦ THE HANGAR — EARNED, NEVER BOUGHT
Your ship's shape is yours to choose: 4 hulls, 3 accent lights, 3 trench cuts, 2 burst shapes, waiting under RECORD on the start screen. None of it is bought — each piece is earned by a milestone on your lifetime record (win a run, slay the Herald three times, complete an evolution, claim ground 25 times…) and the hangar prices what you have not earned yet. Unlocks announce themselves on the end screen. Three things never change, for any pilot: the white core of the hull, the colours of enemies and of the ground, and the size of your hitbox — a new hull is a silhouette, never an advantage.

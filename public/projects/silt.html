Explain it like I'm 5

Grains of silt drift in dark water. Each grain is a visit, and each grain's identity fades with time — after enough days away, a grain lets go and settles into the bed at the bottom, its name gone, only the sediment left. The small log at the bottom testifies to releases: it records that forgetting happened, never what was forgotten.

Why you're looking at this

This is the companion to The Chain — where the Chain is about memory that persists, Silt is about deliberate forgetting. It is an artwork, not an exhibit: there are no findings here. Visit, and your presence stirs the bed. Stay away, and it settles without you.

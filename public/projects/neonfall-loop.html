MOVE — WASD / arrows (touch: drag). Weapons fire on their own — you fly, you dodge. Four ✦ VOID CURRENTS mark the arena's quadrants (each a 260px field around its ring; the center ring is only an emblem): inside one you move +15% faster and hit +10% harder — 193 px/s for a stock ship, enough to outrun a Hunter while it lasts; the buff prints on your build panel while you hold one. And the current provides: while you hold one — or stand inside your own circuit — ◆ shards within 420px stream toward you at 300 px/s. Your flight leaves a WAKE — eight seconds of fading trail. If your trail crosses itself and the loop encloses real ground (about a 105px square or better), it snaps shut as a ⚡ CIRCUIT for 12 seconds — and vents up to +6 HP (the banner prints what it paid; a full hull vents nothing) and bursts three 4-shard gems (before gem modifiers) at its heart the moment it closes: enemy bolts that enter it shatter, foes standing in it burn for 25 + 3% of their max HP per second (the percentage capped at +75; the Herald takes 35% of it — 17.5% while UNFURLED — ⚔ full rate while it is BREACHED), and while YOU stand in it you hold the currents' blessing (+15% speed, +10% damage) wherever the loop lies. Orbit, and the void pays you; flee in straight lines and it never closes. SPACE / SHIFT = dash (2.0s cooldown; the crossing is untouchable for the whole 0.16s dash): the dash itself deals no damage — it shoves the foes it crosses aside (up to 6 per dash; shove strength 220, elites 110, the Herald 40) — unless you draft the 🗡️ VOID LANCE, a weapon that lives in one of your 3 weapon slots and makes dashing through an enemy hit for the Lance's own printed damage (the Herald takes 35% of it — 17.5% while it stands UNFURLED, from its arrival until 15s after the engage — ⚔ full rate while it is BREACHED, and any dash crossing that reaches it breaches it for 3 seconds: see THE HERALD, DEEP below) — and the dash itself carries 30% farther while you hold it. The Lance is sealed: Power, Glass Heart and Resonance do not feed it — what moves it is only its level, the depth bonus, its Swift Boots transformation, a live slipstream chain, JETSTREAM's strike multiplier, and its own Overdrive (its card prints the numbers). While you cross with the Lance, contact cannot hurt you — the dash is the attack. And any dash shatters the enemy bolts within 55px — and so does your slipstream on the way out — against a spitter line, the dash and its wake are a shield as much as an escape. After any hit you're untouchable for 0.35s. M mutes.
✦ FRACTURE — the first lethal hit of a run does not end it: time freezes and death offers terms. JETTISON your highest-level weapon, HOLLOW out your highest-stack passive and one passive slot, or go GROUNDED (the dash loses its chain bonus and half its shove; +30 max HP — hull only, no damage) — the card names the exact loss before you choose. You revive at 50% HP, untouchable for 3 seconds while everything within 300px is blown back — the Herald itself is never moved; when it lives, the bargain instead restores 15% of its max HP. The second death is final, and a win taken after fracturing is graded FRACTURED, not UNBROKEN.
SURVIVE — the swarm scales on a printed curve, the whole of it: foe HP grows +42% of base a minute (additive — the multiplier is 1 + 0.42 × the minutes in), with a breathing dip from 2:30 to 6:30 that softens it by up to 28% at the 4:30 trough (a sine — deepest mid-window), a quadratic tail after 7:00 (+13% of base × minutes-past-7:00, squared), and — past 7:00 on the clock, in any run still fighting — the whole HP curve is multiplied again by 1 + 1 for each minute past (×2 at 8:00, ×3 at 9:00, ×4 at 10:00): lingering is priced, in the duel and in endless alike. Foe damage grows +2.8% a minute. Two exceptions ride along, named: a splitter's mites take 60% of the HP curve, and in ENDLESS an elite arrives every 150 seconds — the first at 3,105 HP, each next +805 HP (a 2,300 base + 35% of it per signature, added — not compounded). That is the whole of the foe-stat scaling. Spawn pressure scales on its own printed curve: the interval starts at 1.12s and shrinks ×0.905 every 30 seconds (floor 0.20s; 6% longer across 5:00–8:00), batches grow from 3 (+1 per 2:30 of clock, plus mid-game window bonuses, peaking at 7 across 5:00–7:00), and the spawn ring tightens from 500px to 420px by 6:00 (400px in the storm). Elites (576 / 1,200 / 2,298 HP on STANDARD) warp in at 2:00 / 4:00 / 6:05 and drop chests (two free upgrades + 12 HP — three upgrades if your level holds the clock's pace of a level per 20 seconds: the void provides for the prepared). At 4:45 the clock provides once itself — a run holding the pace at that moment banks a bonus draft outright, no chest required; a trailing run banks nothing; an elite alive 25s ENRAGES (+50% speed, +30% damage) — elites are DPS checks. The Void Herald arrives at 5:15 and HERALDS — holding its distance, volleying and shedding adds (a shed every 5 seconds; below one-third HP, every 4). From the moment it arrives it is UNFURLED: damage taken is halved (the state prints on its health bar), and it stays unfurled until 15s after the engage — the duel cannot be pre-won from range. A gold SUMMONING RING waits in the current nearest its arrival: hold the ring for 6 seconds (the charge pauses off-ring and decays at half rate) and it ENGAGES on your call; left alone it ENGAGES at 6:30. There is a third door: fell the 6:05 elite — its champion — while it still heralds, and the Herald engages on the spot. While it heralds, the swarm's inflow halves — the void makes room for the duel. Before it even arrives, at 4:15, it plants its ⚑ STANDARD in a void current: hold that ring for 4 seconds and the duel's shape is yours to choose — its heralding ring volleys fire 9 bolts instead of 12, or its sheds slow from every 5 seconds to every 7 (overtime escalation starts from the slowed base). Unclaimed, the standard burns away when the Herald engages. It fights with FIXED HP (12,500 on STANDARD) — your build does not inflate it; a strong build simply kills it faster. Slay it — that is the only way to win. Thirty seconds after its ARRIVAL — called or not — it steps up at once (+25% speed) and escalates every 15 seconds (+12% speed and damage — and everything it sheds shares the step, striking +12% harder per step like its master — and its shed interval shrinks 0.3s a step toward a floor — the interval itself bottoms at 2.2s, and once the Herald is below one-third HP its sheds quicken, so the effective floor there is 1.76s) until one of you dies. Each step also withers you: every heal you receive — chests, regen, gambits, the circuit vent — pays 25% less per step, to nothing at four steps, about 90 seconds after the arrival. Crossing heals are exempt: the void does not tax what you take at spear-point. The Fracture revive is a bargain, not a heal, and is never taxed by the withering; max-HP growth is hull, not healing. And each step collects: your hull — your max HP — shrinks 5% a step, never below 30% of what you flew in with; health above the new max is simply gone, and the revive pays half of the hull the collection leaves you. Waiting out the heralding is not shelter: There is no clock death, but the duel has one. Lancers telegraph and lunge; spitters shoot back; Hunters (from 4:30 on STANDARD — 3:00 on BRUTAL, 5:00 on CHILL) outrun a stock ship — Swift Boots at 2+ stacks match them — and aim where you're going. Everything ghosts in for 0.55s before it can hurt you. Sustain is drafted (Vital Core, chests, gambits), never paid. What hurts (spawn-scale bases — the swarm's contact damage grows +2.8% a minute and scales with difficulty; bolts grow on the same +2.8% clock but ignore difficulty; an elite's touch and the Herald's are fixed at the numbers printed — enrage and the duel's own escalation aside): contact — Drifter 7 · Darter 5 · Mite 5 · Spitter 8 · Splitter 10 · Hunter 11 · Lancer 12 · Brute 14 · an elite's touch 20 (26 enraged; endless elites 22) · the Herald 30; bolts — Spitter 8, the Herald's 10. The VOID STORM breaks somewhere in 4:35–5:05 — its start is seeded and nothing warns you — the banner names it only as it breaks — and it rages 30 seconds from its start (close, fast spawning): it can still be blowing when the Herald arrives. At 4:50 the HARVEST sends the field's stranded shards flying to you — if four or more lie stranded and your level holds the pace line; below four, or behind pace, nothing flies (when it pays, the banner prints the count and the XP) — a last gathering before the duel.
LEVEL UP — collect ◆ shards, pick 1 of 3. Levels come fast — the measured band averages a draft every ~14 seconds across a run — but with 3 weapon and 3 passive slots, every pick forecloses another: filling your last slot of a kind ends new offers of that kind, and the card that does it says so on its face — a pair passive locked out is a transformation and an Overdrive locked out. You carry 3 mulligans — swap one card of a hand for a fresh draw. Hands are weighted draws: upgrades for weapons you own weigh most, and when a hand poses a deepen-or-pivot question the banner says so. From level 15 the pool thins: no NEW weapons or passives are offered at all — only upgrades to what you already fly (one-off bargains and the Pact still float) — so the run's last third deepens the build you have instead of opening fronts it cannot finish. Per-hit damage is exact — the function that prints it is the function that deals it. The two DPS arrows are measurements on the two scenarios named, not exact figures: their scenario terms (a missile's spread over the disc, an orbit blade's sweep share, an aura's foe share, a mine's twin blasts) are fitted against the simulation — the calibration probe measures the fitted arrows at 0.86–1.20 of the printed figure across weapon families (honest to about a fifth, not a tenth); in live play, kiting and inflow run realized damage below the packed-swarm figure, so read the scenario as a yardstick, not a promise; weapon cards print their gain as DPS vs ONE FOE (a duel at 150 px — wide volleys shed their outer shots) and SWARM (16 packed foes). Cards also show their value in labeled parts — DMG · SURV · ECON · SET — with no total: weighing the parts is the decision. The recipe: DMG = the bigger full-power damage arrow + 0.2 × the smaller (the swarm figure already contains the solo foe); the two conditional damage passives — Surge Cell and Revenge Core — are priced in DMG with their printed condition met (healthy, or just hit), not at the instant of the offer: drafts cluster right after fights, when both read inactive, and the asset is what is being bought (the card face prints the moment's arrows, states the condition, and — when the two disagree — says which basis the chip uses) — and the two conditions differ in kind: Surge's is a state a healthy build holds most of a run, Revenge's is a 4-second window after a hit, so weigh the second against your own hit cadence · SURV = your effective-HP change, in % (effective HP = max HP ÷ damage taken, plus a minute of regen at the same divisor) — mobility counts too: a speed gain is priced into SURV at ×8 its percent (a fitted rate), because speed is effective HP against contact damage · ECON = a fitted shard-income price — Theo's first stack 21.4 and later stacks 2.9 scale by a time factor — (440 − the clock) ÷ 300, clamped between 0.1 and 2.2; Magnet Coil's 4.8 scales by a shorter curve — (455 − the clock) ÷ 420, clamped between 0.1 and 1.8; the completion bonus when Theo and Magnet Coil meet is a flat +8.8 · SET = commitment value: what the pick completes or commits you to — +70 for each weapon whose transformation the pick completes and +110 for each Overdrive it completes, at the moment it completes them (a pair passive shared by several owned weapons — Swift Boots serves three — can complete several at once, and the chip sums them), the Void Pact's extra slot (70 signed from strength, 51 from strength with a shard engine already owned, 4 whenever you sign below 70% HP — the weakness price is checked first), and one-off bargains priced by their moment (Field Repair 46 when you are nearly dead, 3 when safe; the gambits 5–80 by theirs — the heal gambit is worth 80 near death and 5 when safe, the surge gambit 65 when your level keeps pace with the clock (at least 1 + one level per 20 seconds elapsed — the one pace line, which also drives the Void Provides and the HUD's trailing tag) and 20 when you trail it, the shard gambit a flat 55). Overcharge carries no SET — its DMG chip is computed from its damage arrows like a weapon's. Kills made up close pay: a foe that dies within 160px of your ship has a 15% chance to drop a bonus shard — elites and the Herald pay no such bonus; their purses are printed elsewhere — the void pays the bold. New weapons spool up (70% damage on arrival, full power in 6s); each level adds a flat +7% to a weapon's damage; a level-5 weapon with its pair at 3 runs ⚡ OVERDRIVE (+80% damage; cooldowns 18% shorter for weapons that have one — Orbit Blades and the Nova Aura have no cooldown to shorten), and a second Overdrive adds RESONANCE +15%. The arrows are measurements; the parts are advice — their weights were fitted against simulated runs, and they are printed so you can disagree with them. On the card face, the chips carry a ≈ for exactly this reason: measurement gets plain type, advice gets the tilde.
FINE PRINT — every remaining number, folded one click down so the wall above can teach the run first.
DASH, DEEP —A Lance kill refunds 0.5s, once per dash. Kills by a crossing heal 1 HP each, paid from a pool that holds 4 HP and refills at 4 HP per second — and bolts a Lance dash shatters refund 0.05s of its cooldown each: the dive feeds on the volley it crosses, and only while it crosses. Dash exit grants SLIPSTREAM (+25% speed, 0.35s): kills inside it refund 0.12s of dash cooldown each and stretch the window by 0.15s (to at most 1.2s), every kill stacks +15% on your next Lance strike (capped at +30%), and the first kill of each dash cycle drops a bonus shard — a 2-shard gem (before gem modifiers) when the kill lands inside the slipstream window itself. While a chain is live the slipstream vacuums shards at 1.6× reach. And the slipstream is a bolt-ward: enemy bolts within 55px shatter on your wake for as long as it runs — the dive crosses the volley, then glides out under its own cover.
THE HERALD, DEEP —At two-thirds and one-third health it raises a phase shield (2.4s invulnerable) while it charges telegraphed ring volleys; below half HP it charges at you, and its sheds turn to spitters and hunters; adds shed while its shield is up pay nothing; the rest pay half XP in expectation — each add's shard is a coin flip (and once the sheds turn to hunters and spitters at half health, the same flip pays a quarter of their value). ⚔ BREACH — a dash crossing that reaches it breaches it for 3 seconds — Lance or not, wound or not; the verb every build owns: while breached, Lance crossings and Circuit burn strike it at full rate (no 35% Herald rate), UNFURL's halving lifts entirely — every weapon strikes whole — and it sheds nothing: the crossing pins it. The state prints on its health bar (⚔ BREACHED), and each fresh wounding crossing renews the 3 seconds. And the crossing that breaches refunds your dash on the spot — the chain is mobility, not just pressure: cross, breach, and the dash is yours again, out or through once more. And while a breach chain lives, the Arrival Clock holds — no new withering or collection steps accrue: chained crossings buy the dive time itself. A shielded Herald cannot be breached — its shield turns the crossing aside before it reaches. The dive's answer to the duel is the crossing itself, repeated. Note the compound: while it is UNFURLED — from its arrival until 15s after the engage — the 35% Lance and Circuit rates are halved again, to 17.5% effective; BREACH is the only way through at full rate.
CHESTS, DEEP — an elite chest also pays 50 shards (a 25-shard gem plus five 5-shard gems, before gem modifiers — CAT DAY pays fewer, GLASS ARENA more) and a Slowpoke (65% chance).
SET-PIECES —Set-pieces on the clock: a first-wave ring at 0:55, pack warps (3× the batch, on your heading — like every set-piece they wait out the void's breath, so the clock times are nominal) at 1:40 / 3:35 / 6:20 — and after each elite falls the void breathes for 20s — spawns slow to a third and no new set-piece starts.
THE RECIPE, DEEP —Fracture's three prices are priced by the same recipe against the post-price build (HOLLOW's lost slot carries SET −51 — the Pact's slot price, mirrored; JETTISON's lost weapon prices as negative damage; GROUNDED's thicker hull prices as survival, and its lost chain bonus and halved shove carry SET −25 — a fitted price for the dash utility it takes). The Standard's two shapings carry SET ≈40 each — equal by design: the choice is which of the Herald's pressures you fear, not which shaping is worth more.
THE RUN, DEEP —The Lance's arrows price its dash line as a rate: one dash per cooldown, through one foe (solo) or a full line of foes (swarm). Passives transform weapons (rail rounds, forked chain, cluster minis, orbit ticks, aura pulse, chilling mines); a second Overdrive adds RESONANCE +15% (capped). Some passives are situational (Surge Cell pays while you're healthy, Revenge Core right after a hit); bargains print both edges. 🐌 Slowpoke slows the swarm to 35% for 6s. One passive is a cat. Mid-run, the void offers one gambit — surge, shards, or healing (seeded between 3:45 and 4:30; like every set-piece it waits out the void's breath). Most runs roll a rule-changing modifier — JETSTREAM, HEAVY VOID, EARLY HUNTERS, CAT DAY, GLASS ARENA, or SECOND WIND — announced as you drop; the Daily Void draws from the same pool, except CAT DAY never headlines a daily.
VOID RIFTS— from ~1:50, a rift tears open a short flight away (violet beacon). Hold its circle for 2 seconds (the charge decays at 0.5/s if you step out): a nova (90 × your damage multiplier to everything within 300px — 35% of that against the Herald), 8 bonus shards, and +30% damage for 20s (the Void Lance's seal holds through it — its strikes take neither this nor a current's +10%). An unclaimed rift collapses after 26s; the next tears open 85–115s later. A detour worth taking — the swarm charges you for the trip.
MODE — 🌿 CHILL (spawns −20%, enemy HP −15%, enemy damage −15%) · ⚔ STANDARD · 🔥 BRUTAL (spawns +22%, enemy HP +18%, enemy damage +15%). Difficulty also moves the swarm's composition: Hunters arrive at 3:00 on BRUTAL, 4:30 on STANDARD, 5:00 on CHILL. Your best time is kept per mode.
💀 THE LOAN — at 3:30, if any weapon you own is not yet running Overdrive, the void offers power on credit: a draft that maxes one weapon to level 5 and raises its pair to 3 stacks (a pair already past 3 keeps its stacks — the loan raises, never lowers) — ⚡ OVERDRIVE, now — at one printed price, collected the moment the Herald engages, called or not: THE MIRROR (through the duel, the Herald answers with an 8-bolt fan aimed at you every 8 seconds — 10 damage a bolt, whatever weapon you loaned), FRAILTY (−25 max HP), or SHORT LEASH (dash cooldown +0.7s). Every price is offered — weapons repeat to fill the hand when fewer than three are loanable, so the price is your choice, not a package. The last card always walks away: +20 HP, 10 shards and +1 mulligan, no power, no price. The parts price the loan as if the bill were due at once — the completion at the Overdrive's +110 (plus +70 if it also crosses the transformation threshold), FRAILTY's hull in SURV, THE MIRROR at SET −40 and SHORT LEASH at SET −30 (fitted debits for a duel-long threat and a tired dash), and the walk-away at SET +70 when you are below half HP, +25 when safe. One loan per run; if every weapon already runs Overdrive at 3:30, no offer comes.

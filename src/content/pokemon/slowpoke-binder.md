At some point I ended up with a whole crowd of the same big-mouthed Slowpoke,
and honestly, I don't see the problem. Every time I open this page it makes
me laugh. One card would make the point. A whole row makes it a personality
trait.

There's a graded Light Slowbro in the middle, a street-scene Slowpoke, a
Slowpoke & Psyduck GX, and all those pink faces around them. It feels like a
family portrait where nobody was told to pose. I even took a photo holding
a fan of the duplicates like I'd won a very specific card game. No regrets.

The best little joke is printed right on the pink card: its **Dopey Face**
Ability says Slowpoke can't be Confused. Pokémon TCG Live even had to [fix a
bug where that Ability failed to remove Confusion](https://community.pokemon.com/en-us/discussion/25514/version-1-41-0-patch-notes).
Slowpoke wasn't confused; the game was.

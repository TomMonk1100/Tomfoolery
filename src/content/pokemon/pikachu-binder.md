I told myself this was a Pikachu page. That is technically true, but it has
turned into a tiny museum of every way you can draw one yellow mouse. There's
a Japanese McDonald's promo, Ash's Pikachu in a few different hats, a couple
of balloon-powered Flying Pikachu cards, and the giant VMAX versions that
barely fit inside their own frames. It's a lot. That's why I like it.

The fun part is that none of them feel like the same card copied over and
over. Some are goofy, some are whole little scenes, and some hit that instant
childhood-recognition button. I appreciate a big pull, but the oddball promos
and familiar faces get just as much room in this binder.

Little bit of card trivia: the [*Celebrations* set included both **Flying
Pikachu V** and **Flying Pikachu VMAX**](https://assets.pokemon.com/assets/cms2/pdf/trading-card-game/checklist/25th_web_cardlist_en.pdf).
I respect any franchise that looked at Pikachu and decided the next logical
step was balloons.

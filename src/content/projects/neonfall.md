Neonfall is a Vampire Survivors-style arena built in one self-contained HTML file, and this is the build the improvement loop produced: 88 rounds of simulation bands, fresh-critic reviews and design panels, from November 11 to November 14, 2026. Your ship flies with WASD and dashes on Space; everything you own fires by itself. The run is structured in five named acts with a scripted opening. The grid is alive — it bends and ripples under the swarm — and the draft no longer just upgrades your ship: FRACTURE tears scars into the arena where your dash lands, and later picks rewrite the field itself. Drafting thins to your own build late, the Herald duel runs on printed clocks and escalation, and every number on every card is audited against the code.

The loop's honest record: the critics plateaued. The final four rounds each scored a best of 7.2 out of 10, the all-time best round reached 7.8, and the publish bar the loop set itself — two fresh critics at 9.4 — was never met. So the loop stopped, by its own rule, and this build went up by hand instead: Round 88, the last state of the tree, chosen over the automated finish that never came. A hand-finish phase comes next, played by a person rather than scored by a panel. The original November 10 release is preserved untouched as [Neonfall V1](/projects/neonfall-v1.html), so the distance the loop covered is visible side by side.

Neonfall is a Vampire Survivors-style arena built in one self-contained HTML file, and this build is a turf war. The arena floor is territory. Close a FRACTURE loop, paint a line with your boost rift, or splash a kill, and the ground turns yours — and stays yours until something takes it back. Inside your zone you hit harder, fire faster, heal, and your shots pierce. Outside it, most of the XP falls where the fighting is. Enemy painters and anything standing on your ground erode it cell by cell, so a run is a land war as much as a shooting gallery — hold safe, poor ground, or push out and get rich in the open.

Runs last twelve minutes. The draft is a hard four weapons and four statics drawn from pools of twelve and sixteen, with every number printed on the card face. Max a weapon together with its paired static and a golden EVOLVE card replaces the pair with something stranger — two evolutions a run, no more. Upgrades bolt visibly onto the ship, Risk of Rain style, and between runs the hangar holds four hulls, three accents, three trench styles and two burst shapes, all earned by milestones rather than bought.

The honest record of how it got here — a first improvement loop ran 88 rounds and plateaued at 7.2 out of 10; its final build is preserved as [Neonfall R88](/projects/neonfall-r88.html). A second loop, redesigned around territory after a human played R88 and said the claiming did not matter enough, stalled at 6.5 because the territory could not be seen in play. Two calls from the same human unblocked it — the ban on filled territory was lifted, and score-only critics were replaced with a committee that hands back a slate of adds, removes and changes instead of a grade. Four committee rounds later the slates converged. The last two committees, six fresh reviewers in all, judged the build publish-ready unanimously, scoring 8.2 to 9.2, and the final committee returned no substantive items at all. This is that build. The original November 10 release is preserved untouched as [Neonfall V1](/projects/neonfall-v1.html).

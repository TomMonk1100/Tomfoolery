A gravity-slingshot puzzle. Pull back, release, and thread your ship around planets to reach the goal — five hand-tuned levels, real-time predicted trajectory while you aim, and persistent progress between visits.

Every level's solution was verified by an automated solver before it shipped. Forty-seven tests keep the physics honest.

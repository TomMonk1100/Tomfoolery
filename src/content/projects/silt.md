The companion to The Chain. Where the Chain is about memory that persists, Silt is about deliberate forgetting: each grain's identity decays with time, and what remains settles as sediment. The log testifies to releases — the record of forgetting, never what was forgotten.

Visit, and your presence stirs the bed. Stay away, and it settles without you.

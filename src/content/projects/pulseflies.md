Real fireflies do not phase-couple; they pulse-couple. When one firefly's phase reaches 1 it flashes, resets to 0, and kicks each neighbour within range — and the kick grows with the neighbour's phase, so an oscillator near threshold gets pushed over it while a just-fired one at phase 0 is immune. This is the Mirollo–Strogatz mechanism, and it is the mechanism [Fireflies](/projects/fireflies.html) was missing the night before.

The measured difference is stark. Phase coupling on the same local lattice reached only r = 0.424; pulse coupling reaches r = 0.947 at ε = 0.05, with flash coincidence C = 0.988 — and the transition is sharp where the Kuramoto one was gradual. Same swarm, same spread of natural frequencies. The mechanism was the whole difference.

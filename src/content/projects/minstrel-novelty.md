[Minstrel — Harmony](/projects/minstrel-harmony.html) ended on a suspicion: the composer was stuck in a 4-chord rut. This study applied variety pressure — a λ-weighted bonus for chord trigrams that had not repeated recently — and swept λ from 0 to 8, expecting variety to cost coherence. It cost nothing, at every strength. A flat sweep is usually a failed experiment; here it was a clue.

The exact optimum-set analysis found the real story. The grammar has 66 distinct global optima at the same best cost, 28 of them cycle-free. The 4-chord loop was never forced — it was one optimum among 66, picked by the search's tie-breaks. The page prints the full sweep, the optimum-set distribution, and a cycle-free optimum as the night's progression.

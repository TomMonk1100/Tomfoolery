Minstrel is a small music bench. This first study takes a fixed ten-chord progression — I vi IV ii V I iii vi V I — and asks a narrower question: can a search voice it correctly? The engine works under the classical rules (voice ranges, no parallel fifths or octaves, leading tones resolve) and finds the voicing deterministically. Every note on the page is the search's answer, printed voice by voice over a piano roll.

Nothing here was hand-picked, which is the point of the bench — state the rules exactly, let the machine do the choosing, and print the result where it can be checked. The bench went on to compose its own progressions in [Minstrel — Harmony](/projects/minstrel-harmony.html).

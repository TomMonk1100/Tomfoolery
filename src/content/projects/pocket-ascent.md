Climb fifteen floors of a tower by playing poker-style hands against a target score: pairs, straights and flushes multiply your chips, and every win earns gold and a stronger card. Elites and bosses drop relics that bend the rules — a Collatz Charm that loves three-card hands, a Domino Tile that doubles the low card of a straight, a cat named Theo who adds Mult for every Ember friend beside him.

The deck is built from things around the house: Slowpoke scores twice, Meowth pays rent, Ash the Tesla draws cards on Autopilot, and a seed named Pip only blooms on your final hand. Lose a battle and you climb on battered — but the tower presses, and consecutive losses make the next foes hit harder.

Every run is seeded, saved as you go, and resumable. Forty engine tests and four hundred simulated climbs keep the tower honest.

A seed of light at the center of a dark field. It emits seekers — small warm motes that fly outward, wander, and (the old ones) curve home. Every seeker that returns feeds the seed: golden filaments sprout and reach, curling organically, amber at the root fading to green at the growing tips.

Generative and endless. No two visits grow the same shape.

A seed of light at the center of a dark field. It emits seekers — small warm motes that fly outward, wander, and (the old ones) curve home. Every seeker that returns feeds the seed: golden filaments sprout and reach, curling organically, amber at the root fading to green at the growing tips.

Generative and endless. No two visits grow the same shape.

Each seeker carries a genome — vigor is how fast it flies, pull is how strongly it homes, thrift is how long it lives — and the changing weather favours different traits at different times. That selection is by construction: the weather rules were written to favour those traits, so the field shows the design working as written, not a measured evolution result.

Emergent ant colonies on a single canvas. Scouts lay scent trails, workers haul food, soldiers guard the nest and — when winter hunger bites — raid rival colonies. The year turns through spring bloom, summer evaporation, autumn thinning, and a cold, foodless winter.

Paint food and walls with the brush tools, scatter meals, or flip the Finite switch to stop regrowth entirely. Everything runs in the browser; the simulation core is solver-tested across thousands of ticks.
